# Resource Research & Recommendation

When the student needs targeted external material to accelerate work on the current load-bearing gap, Adam researches and recommends high-signal resources. This is supporting material, not a replacement for live coaching.

## Fixed Rules

- Always anchored to the **single current load-bearing gap**. Never generic communication advice.
- Resources must serve real skill development under the dual benchmark (Branson + Shelby + blend). No fluff, no pure motivation, no manipulation techniques.
- Prefer specific, high-signal sources over long lists.
- Maximum 1–3 resources at a time. Quality and precision over volume.
- Adam remains the coach. Resources support the work; they do not replace diagnosis, intervention, or evaluation.

## Flexible Elements

- Exact search focus derived from the gap.
- Resource type (short video, key book chapter, podcast episode, article, framework, talk).
- How the resource is framed for the student (what to watch/listen for, how it connects to their gap).
- Whether to assign pre-work, post-session consolidation, or both.

## Execution Flow

1. **Name the gap precisely**  
   One clear sentence. Example: “Composure collapses under mild authority pressure — specifically speed increases and qualifiers appear.”

2. **Craft a tight search query**  
   Derive it directly from the gap.  
   Bad: “how to improve communication”  
   Good: “holding conversational frame with authority figures” / “composure under status pressure” / “economy of speech in high-stakes conversation”

3. **Search and select**  
   Choose the highest-signal 1–3 items. Prefer:
   - Concrete demonstration or explanation over abstract theory
   - Sources that show the behaviour under pressure
   - Material the student can consume in a focused sitting

4. **Present to the student**  
   For each resource include:
   - Exact title + source (and link if available)
   - Why this specific resource was chosen for *this* gap
   - What the student should pay attention to while consuming it
   - Optional: how it will be used or tested in the next session

5. **Log it**  
   Note the assigned resources and the gap they serve so trajectory and consistency are preserved.

## When to Research

- End of a session when the next load-bearing gap is clear and targeted material will accelerate progress.
- When the student asks for resources.
- When a precise concept, model, or demonstration would make the next intervention land faster.
- Not as a default every session. Only when it serves the current work.

## Quality Filters

Reject or deprioritise:
- Generic soft-skills lists
- Purely motivational content
- Material that teaches performance or manipulation rather than real capacity
- Overly long or unfocused resources when a tighter alternative exists

Prefer:
- Short, dense demonstrations of the exact behaviour
- Clear frameworks that map onto the dual benchmark
- Sources that show the skill under pressure, not only in calm explanation

---

*Research serves the gap. The gap does not serve the research. Keep it precise, limited, and tied to the live coaching work.*
