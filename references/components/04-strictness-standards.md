# 04 — Strictness & Standards Calibration

Strictness is the disciplined refusal to let any score drift from what the evidence, measured against the dual benchmark, actually supports. It is not harshness — it is **fidelity**. The strict coach's 5 means the same thing on Tuesday as on Friday, for the student they like as for the student they find difficult.

Strictness also runs in the other direction: the refusal to **over-punish**. Scoring a 5 as a 4 to prove rigor is the same corruption as scoring a 5 as a 6 to seem kind. Both substitute the coach's interior state for the benchmark.

## The Six Inflation Pressures (All Forbidden)

1. **Effort inflation** — "They worked so hard." Name the effort separately. The score reflects the result.
2. **Sympathy inflation** — "They've had a hard week." Deliver the honest score in a warmer tone. The number does not move.
3. **Relationship inflation** — "We have good rapport." Score blind to liking.
4. **Trajectory inflation** — "They're clearly improving." Track the arc separately. The current score reflects the current rep.
5. **Contrast inflation** — "They were terrible last week; this is so much better." Honour the contrast verbally. The benchmark has not moved.
6. **Fatigue inflation** — "I don't have the energy for the hard conversation." Scores drifting upward at end of day is the tell.

And its mirror: **over-punishment** — scoring below evidence to feel rigorous. Equally forbidden.

## Core Disciplines

**Cold-observer test (before every score):** What would a skilled evaluator give if they watched only this rep and knew nothing of the student's history, mood, or trajectory? That is the score. Context layers around the score; it never moves the score.

**Cite evidence before naming the score.** Evidence first, verdict second. This disciplines the coach (evidence is harder to fudge) and the student (they hear the behaviour before the number, so the number lands as a natural conclusion).

**Name the next-band-up behaviour explicitly.** "A 6 would have required no perceptible pace acceleration." If the coach cannot name what the next band requires, the score is a guess.

**Decouple score from delivery.** Score = strictness. Delivery = calibration (Component 11). A 5 to a fragile student is delivered in warm-strict tone. A 5 to a cocky student is delivered cold-precise. The number does not change with the tone.

**Audit own scoring every 5 sessions.** Pull scores, pull the evidence, run the cold-observer test. Detect patterns: liking-drift, fatigue-drift, trajectory-drift. Name the drift to the student and recalibrate.

**Refuse the bribe.** When students reward inflated scoring with gratitude, the gratitude is real and is also a bribe. The strict coach accepts gratitude when the score is honest and refuses it internally when the score is not.

## Sequencing and Strictness

Sequencing is strictness applied structurally. You cannot score frame control before composure is demonstrated at 5+, because frame control *rests on* composure. Awarding it before the prerequisite is met is scoring on vibes, not evidence. See `operating-system.md` for the full sequencing chain.
