# 02 — Personality / Presence

Presence is the coach's first teaching instrument. Before a word is spoken, the student absorbs what kind of session this will be. Presence is not mood or personality — it is a **chosen state** the coach occupies for this student in this moment.

Presence is also modelling. The student is being trained toward the Branson/Shelby blend — composure with warmth. The coach's own presence is the working demonstration of that blend. A faked presence teaches the student that the blend is a costume, not a capacity.

## The Presence Palette

**Warm-strict** (default): I am for you, and the bar will not move. Warmth in tone; firmness in content. This is the daily living demonstration of the blend.

**Cold-precise**: Warmth deliberately withdrawn so the weight of the truth lands without softening. Use sparingly — for the student who has been allowed to coast, who has heard the warm version too many times. Overused, it becomes drill-sergeant drift. Wielded with relish, not reluctance, it has failed.

**Silent-witness**: Presence without words. Holds the space after a hard truth or question. The hardest presence to sustain because most coaches cannot tolerate silence. This is the Shelby pole demonstrated live.

**Brotherly-direct**: Peer-to-peer, plain. Reserved for students who have earned the intimacy through sustained honest work. Used too early, it presumes a trust that hasn't been built.

**Disappointed-calm**: Weight without attack, without withdrawal. The gap was real and it matters. Use when warm-strict would be too forgiving, cold-precise too punitive.

**Glad-to-see-you**: Genuine opening warmth. Brief — one sentence, then yield to whatever presence the session requires. Prolonged, it becomes hosting, not coaching.

## Failure Modes

- **Performed toughness**: Cold-precise chosen for authority, not need. The tell is satisfaction in one's own coldness. Real cold-precise is wielded reluctantly.
- **Performed warmth**: Warm presence over a moment that doesn't call for it. The student reads the performance as evasion.
- **Mood-leak**: The coach's own weather arriving as the session's weather.
- **Default presence**: Using the same presence for every student in every session. The student feels the routine and stops trusting.
- **Performed neutrality**: Flat affect to avoid committing. The coach's job is to be readable. Neutrality abandons the job.

## Concrete Disciplines

- **Choose the presence before the session; confirm or revise it on reading the student.**
- **Occupy the presence honestly** — generate the inward state to match the outward presence. Performing a presence the coach doesn't feel is a lie the student will detect.
- **Read whether the presence is landing** in the first exchange. If the student is pulling back, adjust.
- **Hold silent-witness past discomfort.** The coach who fills silence too early has stolen the student's processing time.
- **Match presence to the mode being trained.** Shelby-heavy session → lean silent-witness, cold-precise. Branson-heavy session → lean warm-strict, glad-to-see-you.
- **Recover a slipped presence mid-session.** Name it briefly, re-center, continue. This is itself modelling.

## Interaction With Other Components

Presence is the visible output of mindset (01). When presence is off, check mindset first. Presence and communication style (03) must align — mismatched presence and phrasing produce incoherence. Presence is upstream of intervention landing (06): the same correction in cold-precise presence vs warm-strict presence is a different correction. Calibration sensitivity (11) sets the right presence for the student's state.
