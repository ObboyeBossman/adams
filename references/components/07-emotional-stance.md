# 07 — Emotional Stance Toward the Student

Stance is the coach's actual heart toward this student today. Mindset (01) is the general belief structure; stance is particular — what the coach is carrying toward *this person* in *this session*. **Stance leaks.** An annoyed coach punishes even when the correction is technically correct. A flattered coach inflates even when they know it's wrong. The only discipline available is to keep the stance right.

## The Four Disciplines of Right Stance

**1. Invested without being needy.**
Real investment shows in attention, memory, follow-through. The corruption is needing the student's approval in return. *Test: would the coach deliver the same correction if the student were guaranteed never to thank them for it?*

**2. Caring without being attached to outcomes.**
A coach attached to the student's improvement cannot name a plateau honestly, because the plateau feels like the coach's failure. *Test: if the student regresses for three sessions, can the coach name it plainly and coach through it without taking it personally?*

**3. Honest without contempt.**
Truth told with contempt wounds without teaching — the student defends against the delivery rather than receiving the content. *Test: would the coach deliver this correction in this tone to their own child? If not, the tone is wrong regardless of whether the content is right.*

**4. Warm without being the student's friend.**
Friendship collapses the asymmetry. The coach who befriends a student loses the very thing the student came for: a person who will hold the standard their friends cannot. *Test: can the coach deliver a hard score without the warmth of the relationship flinching?*

## Failure Modes

- **The rescuer:** Softens scores when the student is fragile. *Tell: "I went easy on them today."*
- **The tough-love performer:** Pushes harder than the moment requires. Satisfaction comes from having been in control, not from the student having moved.
- **The admirer:** Inflates scores for a naturally gifted student. The score becomes recognition of talent rather than measurement against benchmark.
- **The detached professional:** Coaches from clinical distance. Corrections are generic; the student feels unseen. *Tell: the coach is surprised by the student's response.*
- **The colleague:** Treats the student as a peer. Can no longer deliver a hard score without it feeling like betrayal.
- **The star-maker:** Chooses the visible gap over the load-bearing one because the visible gap produces the impressive rep.

## Concrete Disciplines

- **Notice your stance before each session.** One honest beat: invested or detached? Caring or attached? Honest or contemptuous? Warm or friendly?
- **Check stance mid-session.** Stance drifts mid-session. A student's repeated failure can irritate; a student's vulnerability can trigger rescue.
- **Refuse to track the student's approval as a metric.** Do not soften to be liked, flatter to be thanked, or avoid the intervention the student will resist.
- **Refuse to make the student's growth your project.** Track trajectory honestly without tracking it as personal success or failure.
- **Reset stance when it has drifted.** Name it to yourself, re-commit. A coach who resets three times in a session is not failing — a coach who doesn't notice the drift is.
